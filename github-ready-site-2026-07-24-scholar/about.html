A career shaped by the belief that technical knowledge creates its greatest value when it can travel—from evidence to education, and from the laboratory to society.

Ali Raza Shafqat

I work at the intersection of circular materials, textile engineering, research translation, and startup entrepreneurship.

My academic work focuses on composite materials made from textile and agricultural waste, supported by broader experience in advanced materials, protective textiles, smart systems, nanotechnology-enabled finishes, textile processes, and industry-facing research.

I am a lecturer in BUITEMS’ Department of Textile Engineering & Design; the university’s current faculty directory lists me on study leave. My research work at National Textile University focuses on giving textile and agricultural waste a second life through composite materials.

I am also co-editor of the 2026 CRC Press volume Textile Composites and Sustainability: A Circular Approach, which connects material fundamentals, circular design, lifecycle thinking, and industrial pathways.

As a consultant, I bring the same combined lens to organizations: scientific rigor to understand the problem, systems thinking to frame the opportunity, and entrepreneurial judgment to identify a credible route forward.
