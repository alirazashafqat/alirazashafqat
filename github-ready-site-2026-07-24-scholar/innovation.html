ReComp Solutions & innovation

Research should have
a life beyond publication.

ReComp Solutions is the entrepreneurial expression of a research agenda: finding credible ways to transform underused material streams into useful value.

Founder & CEO

ReComp Solutions connects circular-material thinking with the discipline of venture building.

Founded in 2024 at the National Textile University Business Incubation Center, ReComp Solutions creates a platform for exploring practical applications, partnerships, and opportunity around textile and agricultural waste.

The venture reflects a broader professional principle: research becomes more powerful when technical feasibility, stakeholder needs, implementation logic, and commercial reality are considered together.
