Research portfolio

Waste is not the end.
It is a material question.

My research examines how textiles, polymers, fibers, and underused material streams can be engineered for circularity, protection, functionality, and better performance.

Research themes

Connected lines of inquiry across materials and textile systems.

01 / CIRCULAR MATERIALS

Waste-to-value composites

Converting textile dead waste and agricultural residues into higher-value polymer composites, recyclable material systems, and interior applications.

  • Circular composite design
  • Textile and agricultural waste valorization
  • Eco-design and sustainability
  • Thermoplastic composite pathways
02 / PROTECTIVE MATERIALS

Performance under pressure

Applied development of lightweight protection, camouflage, repairable armor, and fiber-reinforced systems.

  • Bulletproof and stabproof jackets
  • Antiballistic composite armor
  • Infrared and marine camouflage
  • Military textile applications
03 / SMART & FUNCTIONAL TEXTILES

Function engineered into fabric

Textile systems that communicate, protect, respond, or carry advanced surface functionality.

  • Multifunctional finishes
  • Nanotechnology in textiles
  • Smart communication systems
  • Textronics and smart materials
04 / TEXTILE ENGINEERING

Processes that shape performance

Investigation of spinning, yarn structure, nonwovens, process parameters, productivity, and industrial improvement.

  • Yarn packing density
  • Spinning and clearing parameters
  • Nonwoven calendaring
  • Energy and production studies
