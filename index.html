01 / PROFILE

Research that moves
from lab to life.

Ali is a textile engineer and academic whose work connects advanced materials research with practical, sustainable outcomes. His doctoral research explores how textile and agricultural waste can become high-value composite materials.

Alongside teaching and research, he leads ReComp Solutions, a startup founded to translate material innovation into products and opportunities for a more circular economy.
